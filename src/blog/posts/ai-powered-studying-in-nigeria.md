## Is AI Studying Cheating?

No. AI is a tool — like a textbook, a calculator, or a study group. The key is **how you use it**:

- **Good use:** Generate practice questions, get explanations for confusing topics, identify knowledge gaps
- **Bad use:** Copying AI-generated answers without understanding them

The goal is to **understand**, not just to get the right answer. For more on why practice questions are so effective, see [Why Practice Questions Can Change the Way You Study](/blog/why-practice-questions-can-change-the-way-you-study).

## Getting Started with AI Studying

1. **Start with your own materials** — AI works best when it has your course content to work with
2. **Ask specific questions** — "Explain the Krebs cycle" is better than "Explain biology"
3. **Verify important answers** — AI is powerful but not perfect. Cross-check critical information
4. **Practice active recall** — Don't just read AI explanations. Test yourself on them
