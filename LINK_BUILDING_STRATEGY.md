# Link Building Strategy — scholarscircle.com.ng

## Priority Order

### 1. Internal Linking (Do Now — Free)
- Strengthen internal links so link equity flows from high-traffic pages to others
- Landing page → `/features`, `/pricing`, `/resources`, `/signup`
- `/features` → `/pricing`, `/signup`, `/resources`
- `/resources` → `/features`, `/signup`
- Blog/resource content → link to relevant app features
- Helps Google understand site structure and improves crawlability

### 2. Directory Submissions (Quick, Free)
- **Google Business Profile** — create a listing for the ed-tech platform
- **Nigerian education forums** — Nairaland (Education section), Nigerian Students Forum
- **Startup directories** — Techpoint.africa, Disrupt Africa, BetaList
- **App directories** — Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, Softpedia (PWA listing)

### 3. Content Marketing (Long-term Engine)
- **Study tips blog** — e.g., "How to Use Spaced Repetition for WAEC/NECO/JAMB"
- **Free practice questions** — sample MCQs that students can share
- **Infographics** — "The Science of Spaced Repetition" — highly shareable
- **Guides** — "Ultimate Guide to AI-Powered Studying in Nigeria"
- Other sites will naturally link to useful, free resources

### 4. Outreach & Guest Posting (Scale Once You Have Content)
- **Education blogs** — offer guest posts to Nigerian education blogs (myschool.com.ng, schoolnews.ng)
- **University websites** — reach out to university departments to recommend the tool
- **Ed-tech partnerships** — collaborate with tutoring centers, JAMB prep sites
- **HARO / SourceBottle** — respond to journalist queries about education technology

### 5. Social & Community Links (Ongoing)
- **Social profiles** — Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram with URL in bio
- **YouTube** — short tutorials showing how to use the platform
- **Reddit** — r/nigeria, r/GetStudying, r/college (provide value, don't spam)
- **WhatsApp/Telegram groups** — share with Nigerian student study groups

### 6. Technical SEO Foundations (In Place)
- ✅ Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- ✅ `robots.txt` configured
- ✅ Security headers set
- **TODO:** Add `<link rel="canonical">` tags in HTML `<head>`

### 7. Monitor & Track (Ongoing)
- **Google Search Console** — monitor backlinks, indexing status, search queries
- **Google Analytics** — track which external sources drive traffic
- **Ahrefs Free Backlink Checker** or **Ubersuggest** — monitor backlink profile
